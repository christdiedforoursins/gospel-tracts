You are a sinner and cannot save yourself (Romans 3:23). 
Jesus Christ died on the cross for your sins (Romans 5:8). 
He was buried and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3-4). 
If you believe in Jesus Christ with all your heart, you will be saved (Acts 16:31). 
Salvation is a free gift from God, not something you can earn (Ephesians 2:8-9). 
There is nothing you can do to make yourself more acceptable to God (Titus 3:5). 
Simply trust in Jesus Christ alone for your salvation (Romans 1:17).

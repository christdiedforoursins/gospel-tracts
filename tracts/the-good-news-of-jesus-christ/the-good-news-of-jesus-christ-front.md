# The Good News of Jesus Christ

The word *Gospel* means “Good News.”  
And it truly is the best news you’ll ever hear.

---

## The Problem  
The Bible says that every one of us has sinned. None of us are perfect or righteous on our own  
(**Romans 3:23**, **1 John 1:8**, **Ecclesiastes 7:20**).  
Because of sin, we face spiritual death — eternal separation from God  
(**Romans 6:23**, **John 3:18**).

---

## God’s Solution  
But God didn’t leave us without hope.  
He sent His Son, **Jesus Christ**, who is both fully God and fully man, to pay the penalty for our sin  
(**John 1:1**, **2 Corinthians 5:21**, **Romans 3:25**).  

Jesus died on the cross for your sins, was buried, and rose again the third day — proving His power over sin and death  
(**1 Corinthians 15:1-4**).

---

## The Promise  
God promises that anyone who believes in Jesus — trusting that He alone can save them — will be saved.  
> *“Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.”*  
> — **Acts 16:31**

This salvation isn’t something we earn or deserve.  
It’s God’s free gift — His grace — given to us through faith  
(**Ephesians 2:8-9**, **Romans 5:8**, **Titus 3:5**).

---

## The Assurance  
Once you’ve trusted Christ, you are secure forever.  
Nothing can separate you from His love or take away your salvation  
(**John 10:28–29**, **Romans 8:38–39**, **Ephesians 1:13–14**).  

You are now God’s child, promised eternal life with Him in heaven  
(**John 5:24**, **John 14:2–3**).

---

## The Invitation  
If you believe that Jesus died for your sins and rose again, you can trust His promise right now.  
There are no magic words — only faith in what God has already done for you through His Son.

> *For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.*  
> — **John 3:16**
